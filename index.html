About Me

I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Artificial Intelligence Engineering at Mapúa University, where I also completed my Senior High School under the Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM) strand. My academic journey has cultivated a strong foundation in computational and analytical thinking, leading to my research interest in developing predictive models for financial and economic systems. I have developed proficiency in Python, TensorFlow, and data analytics, with growing expertise in applying machine learning and deep learning techniques to financial time-series data. My recent project, Predictive Modeling of Financial Market Direction Using LSTM-Based Deep Learning Architecture, implements LSTM, GRU, and ensemble models to forecast market trends through technical indicators such as RSI, MACD, and Bollinger Bands. This work reflects my broader aspiration to advance the use of artificial intelligence in enhancing economic forecasting and financial decision-making. With a long-term goal of earning a PhD, I aim to contribute to the growing body of research that bridges artificial intelligence, economics, and finance.

Why AI Engineering? I chose this field because of its transformative potential to model, analyze, and predict complex financial and economic systems through intelligent, data-driven solutions.

Projects

Predictive Modeling of Financial Market Direction Using LSTM-Based Deep Learning Architecture

This project develops an LSTM-based deep learning framework for mid- to long-term prediction of stock market direction. Utilizing OHLCV data obtained from CSV exports or Yahoo Finance, the model integrates a comprehensive suite of technical indicators—including Relative Strength Index (RSI), Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD), Bollinger Bands, Exponential and Simple Moving Averages, and Fibonacci retracements—to enhance the representation of market dynamics. Multiple neural architectures, including LSTM, GRU, and feedforward networks, are implemented individually and as an ensemble to improve predictive robustness. The framework also incorporates systematic model training with early stopping, performance evaluation through backtesting, and visualization of results to support data-driven decision-making in financial forecasting and quantitative research.

Arduino-Based Prototype: Flood Detection System Using Ultrasonic and Soil Moisture Sensors

Developed a flood detection prototype using Arduino, ultrasonic sensors, and a soil moisture sensor to detect rising water levels and ground saturation. The system provides real-time visual alerts using red and green LED indicators to signal potential flood conditions..

Anecdote / Blog

My fascination with Artificial Intelligence began not with coding or data, but with a question: Can machines truly understand the logic behind human decisions in finance? That curiosity led me to explore how algorithms interpret uncertainty, risk, and emotion in markets that often seem unpredictable. What I find most compelling about AI is its blend of precision and imperfection — it mirrors how humans reason, yet exposes patterns we fail to see. Studying its mathematical foundations has been both demanding and rewarding, teaching me that progress in this field requires patience, abstraction, and a tolerance for ambiguity. Beyond the technical challenges, I am drawn to AI’s potential to reshape how we understand economic behavior — not by replacing human insight, but by deepening it through data-driven reasoning.
